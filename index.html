How to read this

Quality, 1 to 5

Large, multimodal, richly annotated, curated (often BIDS), documented and widely reused.
Solid size or unusually good annotations; minor gaps.
Usable but limited: small N, partial annotations or a single modality.
Small, thinly documented, or mostly useful as a benchmark.
Marginal: re-uploads, toy sets, unclear provenance.

Tiers

Tier 1 fits epilepsy network work directly: intracranial or epilepsy imaging with onset zones, outcomes, cavities or same-subject multimodal data. Tier 2 is strong supporting material: controls, normative atlases, single-modality epilepsy cohorts. Tier 3 is peripheral or low quality, listed for completeness.

Method

Each row's details say when and how it was checked and what was corrected. "Confirmed in the files" means someone read the released files themselves; other tags come from the dataset page, README or paper, so confirm details on the dataset page before relying on them.
